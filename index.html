⚠ HOW TO PLAY

☣ OBJECTIVE

Infect every country, then wipe out humanity before a cure is finished. You lose if the cure is deployed, or if your pathogen kills its last host while anyone is still uninfected.

⚡ DNA POINTS

Earn DNA as the outbreak grows, reaches new countries, and from bubbles on the map. Spend it in EVOLVE. Devolving refunds half; random mutations are free and can be devolved.

🧬 EVOLUTION STRATEGY

1. Transmissions decide how you travel: by plane, ship or over land.
2. Keep symptoms low until you are everywhere.
3. Then turn lethal. Too early and it burns itself out.

🌡 CLIMATE & WEALTH

Cold and arid countries resist you until you adapt. Poor countries spread fast; rich ones spread slowly, fund the cure, and close their airports first.

🔬 DETECTION & CURE

Visible symptoms and deaths fill the detection meter. Once identified, research begins and governments start closing routes. Dead and sick researchers cure nothing.

🎯 OPERATIONS

The OPS tab in EVOLVE has one-off actions: smuggle the pathogen into sealed countries, go dormant to hide, stage superspreader events, or stall the cure with disinformation.

🗺 MAP

Orange to red shows infection, near-black shows mass death. Tap a country to see whether the outbreak there is growing or burning out.
